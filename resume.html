Summary

Geospatial leader with 13+ years building, governing, and modernizing enterprise GIS across utility, energy, federal, and intelligence organizations. Currently Capability Lead for Systems and Architecture at Geographic Technologies Group, leading a team of 5 senior GIS analysts and developers.

What drives my work is finding the places where geospatial data answers a question nobody thought to ask. I have done that repeatedly: in transmission engineering, where a live project became the demo that put GIS into engineering, siting, and real estate; at a federal agency, where a program run on spreadsheets and email became an automated enterprise system; and at the Corps of Engineers, where scattered project data became the dashboards leadership used to brief Congress.

I lead the way I learned to in 25 years of Army Reserve service: clear standards, honest communication up the chain, and real investment in the people doing the work. Mentoring is a two-way street for me. I have learned this field by doing it, and the people I mentor regularly teach me approaches I would not have found on my own.
